One line of text, written as the office printer's own comeback to everyone
who jams it, thumps it, and stares at its cryptic error codes — for the
[make.fun bounty "The Printer Finally Answers"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-printer-finally-answers-8fbf0907).

## The entry

> Error 0x0001: I jammed on purpose. Your document was 87 pages long and called 'final_FINAL_v3'.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when run outside the grader).

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
